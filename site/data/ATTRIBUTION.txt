Data sources for the Sutton SignWriting Office

Country codes and English country names are derived from Debian iso-codes, copyright its
contributors, licensed LGPL-2.1-or-later:
https://salsa.debian.org/iso-codes-team/iso-codes

Spoken-language suggestions are derived from Unicode CLDR territory language information,
copyright Unicode, Inc., licensed under the Unicode License v3:
https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json
https://www.unicode.org/license.txt

CLDR population data is approximate and is used only to suggest useful language choices. It is not
an exhaustive statement about the languages of a country.

Sign-language associations are a 2026-09-04 snapshot of curated Sutton SignWriting project data:
data/ref/world/countries.json and data/ref/world/languages.json in the Sutton SignWriting project.
The Office presents this separately from spoken-language suggestions and invites corrections.
