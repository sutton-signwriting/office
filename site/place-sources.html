Map and place sources

Reviewed September 12, 2026. This page is currently in English.

Country overview

The overview uses Natural Earth country geometry at 1:110m. Small places may not appear at this scale; the selectors include the full country list. Regional selections are shown by name, with their country outlined on the map.

Natural Earth provides cartographic boundaries, not a registration census. The map does not show population, membership, or research-interest counts. Geometry is pinned to source commit ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19. Natural Earth's data are public domain.

Place names and codes

Country and subdivision codes come from Debian iso-codes; the subdivision snapshot is version 4.16.0. Country names follow the selected interface language. Subdivision names retain the source spelling. A chosen region is displayed by its own name.

These are dated reference data. Send place-name or map corrections to help@signwriting.org. Place selection provides local context and does not select your interface language.
